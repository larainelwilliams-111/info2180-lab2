My First Survival World

October 3, 2026 by Laraine Williams

Starting a new survival world always feels like a fresh page. I spawned beside a quiet forest, punched my first tree and quickly gathered enough wood for a crafting table and a set of basic tools. Before the sun set I had dug a small shelter into the side of a hill and sealed the entrance.

The next morning I explored the nearby plains, collecting wheat seeds, a few sheep and plenty of stone. By the end of the first week I had a small farm, a mine that reached the first coal veins and a plan for a much larger base by the river. Planning ahead has made the whole game feel far less stressful.

Surviving the First Night

October 10, 2026 by Laraine Williams

Nothing prepares you for the first time a creeper hisses behind you in the dark. I learned quickly that torches are your best friend, because well-lit ground keeps most monsters from spawning near your door. Always carry a few in your inventory before heading out at dusk.

My strategy now is simple: gather resources by day, mine underground by night and never wander far without a sword and some food. It is not a perfect plan, and I still lose a few items now and then, but every mistake teaches me something new about the game and about careful planning.
